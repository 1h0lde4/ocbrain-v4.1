## 3. Disposition

> The digest recorded in the verification report (B) matches the measured bytes. The three records of A do not match the measured bytes: they differ from the measured digest at offset 23, `c` where the measured digest has `f`. If the measured file is the artifact REV3 describes, A is a transcription error at offset 23. The verification report is not rewritten.

A digest identifies the bytes measured. It does not by itself show that those bytes are the historically intended artifact, and a passing internal manifest check does not authenticate the archive; the consistency checks above support, but do not prove, that they are. The original-artifact authentication is therefore recorded as a **qualified** result: bytes measured, digest = B ≠ A, identity as the intended artifact supported and not proven.
