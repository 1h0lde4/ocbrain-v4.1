## 1. Conventions

**1.1 SHAs and targets.** `fbe1ebdef553b52abf270a819d976d2e078feeef` is the **finding baseline** for rows first identified in the baseline report; `cc446f87e72d5f8277da3c293a88ee79a7e872bd` is `main` at this revision. These are not the evidence targets. Each row's **reproduction target**, **candidate/merge/verification target** and **CI target** are separate and are listed in Part C1. A reader must not infer that evidence was produced at the finding baseline.

**1.2 IDs.** `ACC` accepted decision · `DEC` outstanding decision · `DEF` implementation defect · `DOC` documentation row · `EVG` evidence gap · `EV` evidence item · `E` erratum. IDs are immutable: never reused or renumbered. A superseded row stays, marked `SUPERSEDED BY <id>`. IDs here are local and deliberately do not reuse the project register's `B1…B9` batch labels.

**1.3 Closure model.** Each defect row carries four independent fields. None implies another (`PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §0.3, §16.5, §24.2).

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Decision | `ACCEPTED` · `PROPOSED` · `OPEN` · `DEFERRED` · `REJECTED` · `N/A` — status of the governing owner decision(s) for the row (Part A). Criteria ratification is separate: every Part B criterion is PROPOSED and closure also requires the owner's ratification of that row's criteria. |
| Implementation | `NOT STARTED` · `IN PROGRESS` (a candidate exists, unmerged) · `COMPLETE` (merged) · `BLOCKED` · `N/A` |
| Verification | `NOT RUN` · `EXECUTED` (the prescribed procedure was run, for example by the author, but no independent review has checked it against the criterion) · `FAILED` · `PASSED` · `INCONCLUSIVE` · `N/A` — `PASSED` requires qualified evidence (§1.6) and an independent review (`IR`); `EXECUTED` never counts as `PASSED` |
| Reconciliation | `PENDING` · `COMPLETE` · `N/A` — whether the project registers (`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `CURRENT_STATE.md`) match the latest implementation and evidence |

Displayed disposition is **derived**, never typed in:

| Condition | Derived disposition |
|---|---|
| Decision ACCEPTED or N/A, Implementation COMPLETE or N/A, Verification PASSED, Reconciliation COMPLETE, criteria ratified | `CLOSED` |
| As above but Reconciliation not COMPLETE | `VERIFIED, NOT CLOSED` |
| Implementation COMPLETE, Verification NOT RUN, EXECUTED or INCONCLUSIVE | `FIXED, UNVERIFIED` |
| Implementation COMPLETE, Verification PASSED, Decision OPEN or PROPOSED | `NOT ELIGIBLE FOR CLOSURE` |
| Decision OPEN or PROPOSED, or Implementation BLOCKED, with work not yet complete | `BLOCKED` |
| Decision DEFERRED | `DEFERRED` — never closed; the freeze disposition (Part D) needs an explicit owner choice |
| Anything else | `OPEN` |

For an `EVG` row, Implementation is `N/A` and the Verification field is the evidence state. A `DOC` row closes only when the documentation change is merged, checked against the live state and independently reviewed. Every closure-bearing row states its closure conditions: its criteria (Part B) or Required-to-close cell (Part C2), its typed dependencies (§1.4) and any owner decision it needs; a row that omits one of these cannot close.

**1.4 Relationship types** (never mixed): `H` hard dependency (cannot be implemented or accepted without it) · `P` policy dependency (needs an owner decision) · `V` verification dependency (cannot be independently verified until it holds) · `C` coordination order (recommended sequence; work may proceed independently) · `S` shared scope (same subsystem; neither blocks the other).

**1.5 Freeze disposition** (Part D): `MUST FIX` · `OPEN RISK` (not yet dispositioned; blocks pending decision) · `DEFERRED` (postponed, no risk accepted; does not by itself permit a freeze) · `ACCEPTED RISK` (owner accepts a defined residual risk; only under ratified freeze criteria) · `NOT APPLICABLE` (evidence shows the condition does not apply) · `VERIFIED CLOSED`. `DEFERRED` never becomes `ACCEPTED RISK` implicitly. An accepted risk needs: exact affected feature or deployment boundary; risk description; impact and likelihood; compensating controls; named accountable owner; acceptance date; expiry or reassessment date; reopen events.

**1.6 Evidence classes.** **`IR`** = independent review of a pinned candidate SHA by a reviewer or session that did not author it, against the baseline reproduction. Reading the author's output is not an independent re-run: where a row requires a re-run, the reviewer records the exact revision, environment, commands and inputs used, and `PASSED` needs that re-run to produce the required result; an author-run pass stays `EXECUTED`. **`CE`** = CI success plus test-collection evidence plus per-test results (log or artifact showing the named tests were collected and passed, not skipped or deselected). **Provisional CI evidence:** a green workflow or full-suite result that may support a finding but cannot alone establish closure. **Qualified CI evidence:** execution, collection, exclusions and gate behaviour meet the accepted verification requirements. Until DEF-07, DEF-08, DEF-09 and DEF-15 are verified for the outcome class relied upon, all CI evidence is provisional.

**1.7 Evidence tags.** `CONFIRMED` (reproduced or traced in live code) · `STATIC` (read, not executed) · `REGISTER-ONLY` (taken from project registers, not re-checked) · `REPORTED` (supplied by the project side, not retrieved here) · `UNVERIFIED`.

**1.8 Owners and authority.** Implementation owner is `UNASSIGNED` for every row; no assignment is recorded and assigning one is an owner decision. The default closure authority is the project owner, on evidence from `IR`. Decisions close only by the owner's written decision. The dated Nov 2 `docs/engineering/DEBT_BRANCH_OWNERSHIP_REGISTER_2026-11-02.md` is a branch/PR snapshot and was not re-inventoried; Part C3 is a fresh partial inventory.

**1.9 Authority and precedence (PROPOSED).** Two kinds of authority are kept apart. *Evidence authority:* the baseline report is immutable historical evidence; where an erratum (§2) exists, the erratum governs how the baseline is read; newer independently verified evidence (Part C) governs the relevant current factual claim. Evidence updates an evidentiary status under the applicable EVG rules. Such an update does not, by itself, accept a criterion, close a defect, change an owner decision, authorize implementation or merging, or declare freeze readiness. *Owner authority:* acceptance, risk acceptance, authorization and the freeze verdict change only through an explicit written owner decision (§1.8). A revision of this register and any review of it are advisory or PROPOSED until ratified, and never supersede an owner decision. Where artifacts disagree and no erratum covers it, the disagreement is recorded as an open question (§E3) rather than resolved by choosing the more convenient source.
